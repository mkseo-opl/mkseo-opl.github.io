abstract: >
  This study investigates the performance improvement of triglyme-based low-toxicity hypergolic fuels
  using an ionic liquid additive with high test peroxide (HTP) as the oxidizer. Copper-doped triglyme
  formulations were proposed as green alternatives to toxic MMH, with droplet impact experiments used
  to measure ignition delay times. Theoretical performance was analyzed using NASA CEA and compared
  with conventional toxic propellant combinations.
