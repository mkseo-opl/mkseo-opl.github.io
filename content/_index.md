        The **Orbital Propulsion Laboratory (OPL, 궤도추진연구실)** at [Kyungil University](https://www.kiu.ac.kr/) focuses on the development and testing of liquid bipropellant and monopropellant systems for space and defense applications.

        Our research spans **green hypergolic propellants**, **Mars EDL propulsion**, **lunar lander systems**, and **MMH/NTO bipropellant thrusters** — building on over a decade of hands-on heritage from Korea's first lunar orbiter (Danuri) and national satellite programs.
