Minkyo Seo (서민교) is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Kyungil University (경일대학교) and the founder of the **Orbital Propulsion Laboratory (OPL / 궤도추진연구실)**.

His research focuses on liquid rocket propulsion, satellite propulsion systems, and hypergolic propellant chemistry, with an emphasis on MMH/NTO bipropellant systems and HTP-based low-toxicity alternatives.

Prior to joining Kyungil University in 2026, he spent 13 years as a Senior Research Engineer at Hanwha Aerospace, where he contributed to Korea's national space launch program including the KSLV-II main engine development. He received his PhD in Aerospace Engineering from KAIST in 2025.
