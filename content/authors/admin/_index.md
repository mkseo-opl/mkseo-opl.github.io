Minkyo Seo is an Assistant Professor at Kyungil University (경일대학교), where he leads the **Orbital Propulsion Laboratory (OPL / 궤도추진연구실)**. His research focuses on liquid rocket propulsion, satellite and spacecraft propulsion systems, and low-toxicity hypergolic bipropellants for space applications including orbital transfer vehicles (OTV) and lunar landers.

Before joining academia, he spent 13 years at Hanwha Aerospace as a Senior Researcher in the Space Business Division, contributing to Korea's KSLV-II engine development and building Korea's first MMH/NTO combustion test infrastructure. He received his PhD in Aerospace Engineering from KAIST in 2025.
